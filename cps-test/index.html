How the CPS test works

The CPS test measures your clicks per second — how many times you can press the mouse button in a set window of time. Pick a duration, then click the pad as fast as you can. The timer starts on your very first click, so there is no wasted reaction time, and it stops automatically when the clock runs out. Your score is simply the number of clicks divided by the seconds, so 40 clicks in a 5-second test is 8 CPS.

Most people run the 5-second test because it is long enough to be fair but short enough to sprint the whole way. Shorter tests reward a fast burst; longer ones reward stamina. Every duration keeps its own separate personal best on this device.

What is a good CPS score?

The average click speed sits around 6 to 7 CPS with a normal single-finger click. Break past 8 and you are clicking faster than most people; get above 11 and you are in the top few percent. Here is how the ranks on this test line up:

  • Under 4 CPS — Snail to Turtle. A relaxed, everyday click.
  • 6 to 7.5 CPS — Rabbit. Right around the human average.
  • 7.5 to 11 CPS — Cheetah to Falcon. Clearly fast; you have practised.
  • 11+ CPS — Lightning. Usually a special technique, not a plain click.

How to click faster

A normal click tops out for most people around 7 CPS. A few simple habits push it higher:

  • Use short bursts. Your fastest rate only lasts a second or two, so sprint on the shorter tests instead of pacing yourself.
  • Stay loose. Rest your hand on the mouse and let your finger do the work — a tense grip slows you down.
  • Try two fingers. Alternating two fingers on the button, or two thumbs on a phone in the tap speed test, can beat a single finger.

Whatever you do, click in short bursts and stop if anything starts to ache. Speed comes from staying relaxed, not from forcing it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CPS score?

Around 6 to 7 CPS is average. Above 8 is fast, and above 11 puts you in the top few percent of clickers.

What is the average CPS?

On a standard 5-second test the average is roughly 6.5 clicks per second with a normal single-finger click.

How is CPS calculated?

It is your total clicks divided by the length of the test. Click 40 times in 5 seconds and your score is 8 CPS.

Does a faster CPS help in games?

In some games a higher click rate helps with actions like combat or building. This test is a fun way to measure and practise, not a guarantee of in-game results.
