The 20 second click speed test

The 20-second CPS test is an endurance run. Going all-out early leaves your hand tired for the finish, so most people settle into a rhythm they can hold. Your score is total clicks divided by 20, and every 20-second run keeps its own personal best on this device.

What is a good score on the 20 second test?

On this length, around 6.5 CPS is a solid result for a normal click. Shorter tests let you post a higher number from a single burst; longer tests pull your average toward the rate you can truly sustain.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good 20 second CPS score?

About 6.5 clicks per second is solid over 20 seconds. Shorter tests read higher, longer tests lower.

How is the score calculated?

Total clicks divided by 20 seconds. Click 40 times in a 20-second test and that's 2.0 CPS.

Does each duration save its own best?

Yes. Your personal best is stored separately for every CPS length on this device.

Which CPS length is standard?

The 5-second test is the most common and fairest all-round measure of click speed.
