Privacy policy

The short version: SpeedLab keeps your scores on your device and sends nothing to anyone. There is not much to a privacy policy when the site collects nothing.

What we store, and where

When you take a test, your best scores and your settings (like whether sound is on) are saved using your browser's localStorage. That data lives on your own device, in your own browser. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. Clear it any time with the “Clear my scores” button on a test, or by clearing your browser's site data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No forms and no personal data collection.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No third-party embeds or external scripts. Fonts are hosted here, not fetched from anyone else.
