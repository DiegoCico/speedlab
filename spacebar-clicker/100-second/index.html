The 100 seconds spacebar test

The 100-second spacebar marathon. Your score is your presses per second over the full 100 seconds, and this length keeps its own personal best on this device. On a phone, tap the pad instead of pressing a key.

Try other lengths

Every spacebar length has its own page: 5 Seconds · 10 Seconds · 30 Seconds · 1 Minute. Or use the main spacebar clicker, which also has a no-timer mode.

How to press faster

  • Alternate two fingers on the bar instead of one.
  • Match your effort to the clock — burst on short runs, pace the long ones.
  • Stay loose; a tense hand fades fast past 30 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good spacebar speed?

Around 6 to 7 presses per second is average. Above 8 is fast; shorter runs read higher than longer ones.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Tap the pad instead of pressing a key and it counts your taps the same way.

Does each length save its own best?

Yes. Your best is stored separately for every spacebar duration on this device.

Is there a no-timer mode?

Yes, on the main spacebar clicker page you can count presses with no clock at all.
