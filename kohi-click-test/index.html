What is the Kohi click test?

The Kohi click test is a click-speed test named after Kohi, an old and hugely popular Minecraft PvP server where fast clicking gave players an edge in combat. The test itself is exactly the same idea as a normal CPS test — it counts how many times you click in a set number of seconds — but Minecraft players have searched for it by the Kohi name for years. Pick a duration, click the pad as fast as you can, and your speed shows up in clicks per second.

What is a good Kohi CPS score?

A normal click sits around 6 to 7 CPS. In Minecraft PvP, players often aim for 8 or more, and the fastest legit clickers using techniques can push well past that. Here is the rough scale on this test:

  • Under 4 CPS — a relaxed, everyday click.
  • 6 to 7.5 CPS — right around the human average.
  • 8 to 11 CPS — fast; you have clearly practised.
  • 11+ CPS — usually a special technique rather than a plain click.

How to click faster for Minecraft

Beyond a normal click, players use a few techniques to raise their CPS. Each has its own test here so you can practise and measure it:

  • Jitter clicking — tensing your arm so it vibrates for very rapid clicks.
  • Butterfly clicking — alternating two fingers on one mouse button.
  • Drag clicking — dragging a finger across the button so friction registers many clicks.

Whatever style you use, keep your hand loose and click in short bursts. Note that many servers set a CPS limit or ban certain techniques, so check the rules where you play.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Kohi click test the same as a CPS test?

Yes. It measures clicks per second in exactly the same way; the Kohi name comes from a Minecraft PvP server, so players search for it separately.

What is a good Kohi CPS for Minecraft?

Around 6 to 7 CPS is average. Many PvP players aim for 8 or more, and techniques like jitter or butterfly clicking go higher.

Does higher CPS get you banned?

Some Minecraft servers cap CPS or ban autoclickers and certain techniques. This test is just practice; always follow your server's rules.

Which duration should I use?

Short runs like 1 or 5 seconds reward a fast burst; 10 seconds is the common standard. Each duration keeps its own best.
