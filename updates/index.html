What's new on SpeedLab

SpeedLab is actively maintained — new games, guides and fixes land regularly. Here's the running log of what's changed, newest first.

October 2026

  • Eight new guides. Added in-depth articles: what a good reaction time is, how to click faster, how to type faster, reaction time in gaming, hand-eye coordination, can you train your reflexes, working memory, and a gaming warm-up routine. The Guides section now has twelve articles.
  • Suggestion box. You can now suggest a game straight from the contact page.
  • New trust pages. Added a disclaimer and expanded the about page with how scoring and ranks work.

Late September 2026

  • Four new games. Dino Dash (an endless runner that speeds up), 2048, Clusters (sort 16 words into 4 groups), and Word Spammer (type your own word as fast as you can).
  • Guides launched. The first written guides went live, explaining the metrics behind the tests.
  • Polish. A homepage intro animation, arcade-style navigation, and a merge animation for 2048.

Mid September 2026

  • Seven new brain & reflex games. Mental Math Sprint, Stop the Clock, Memory Match, Count the Flash, Go / No-Go, Same or Mirror, and Word Unscramble.

September 2026 — launch

  • SpeedLab went live with the core tests: CPS, spacebar, reaction time, typing, aim trainer, the memory suite, and more — all free, no sign-up, scores saved on your device.
