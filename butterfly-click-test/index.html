What is butterfly clicking?

Butterfly clicking is a technique where you use two fingers on a single mouse button, tapping them one after the other so fast that the clicks blend together. Because two fingers alternate, each one only has to move at half the rate to double your total click speed — which makes butterfly clicking one of the fastest methods that still feels comfortable. It is popular in Minecraft PvP and other click-heavy games.

It is gentler on your hand than jitter clicking, but some mice register the doubled clicks unevenly, so results can vary by mouse.

What is a good butterfly click speed?

  • Under 10 CPS — you are getting the hang of the rhythm.
  • 12 to 14 CPS — solid, consistent butterfly clicking.
  • 15 to 18 CPS — fast and well-timed.
  • 18+ CPS — excellent; you have really dialled in the two-finger rhythm.

How to butterfly click

  1. Place two fingers — usually your index and middle — on the same mouse button.
  2. Tap them alternately: one down, then the other, in a steady one-two rhythm.
  3. Start slow to lock in the alternation, then speed the rhythm up. Even timing beats raw force.

Frequently asked questions

What is butterfly clicking?

Butterfly clicking uses two fingers alternating on one mouse button, so the clicks blend together and your total speed roughly doubles.

Is butterfly clicking better than jitter clicking?

It is usually faster and easier on your hand than jitter clicking, but some mice register the doubled clicks unevenly, so it depends on your gear.

What is a good butterfly click CPS?

Around 12 to 14 CPS is solid. 15 to 18 is fast, and above 18 shows an excellent two-finger rhythm.

Does butterfly clicking work on any mouse?

Most mice handle it, but cheaper switches can miss or double-count clicks. A mouse with clean, responsive switches gives the most consistent results.
