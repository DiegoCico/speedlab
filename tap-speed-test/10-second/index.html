The 10 seconds tap test

The standard tap sprint. Your score is your taps per second over the full 10 seconds, and this length keeps its own personal best. It works with a finger on a touchscreen or a mouse on a computer.

Try other lengths

Other tap lengths: 5 Seconds. Or use the main tap speed test.

How to tap faster

  • Use two fingers or thumbs alternating like a drum roll.
  • Rest your device on a surface so your hand is free to move.
  • Keep taps light and quick rather than hard.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good tap speed?

About 6 to 7 taps per second with one finger is average. Two fingers can push well past 10.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes. Clicking the pad with a mouse works exactly like tapping.

Does each length save its own best?

Yes. Your best is stored separately for every tap duration on this device.

Is tapping the same as CPS?

It's measured the same way and lands in a similar range; tap speed is the touchscreen name for it.
